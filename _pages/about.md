I am a computational anthropologist who studies the anthropology ***of*** and ***with*** computation. I investigate how cultural meaning is encoded in everything from archaeological archives and global museum collections to digital platforms, marketplaces, and contemporary AI systems, and I build computational methods that connect local meaning-making to the large-scale information infrastructures that now mediate cultural life. For me, "scale" is not simply a technical issue but an important site of anthropological inquiry: understanding culture today requires tracing how small, situated practices become entangled with computational systems operating at planetary scope.

I earned my PhD in Anthropology at Harvard University and am currently teaching Computational Social Science at the University of Chicago.
